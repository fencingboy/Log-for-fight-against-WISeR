Understanding the WISeR Model & Rep. Suzan DelBene's Cause

What is the WISeR Model?

The Wasteful and Inappropriate Service Reduction (WISeR) model is a Medicare pilot program implemented by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). It incorporates artificial intelligence algorithms into Traditional Medicare prior authorization decisions for targeted outpatient medical procedures—including nerve stimulators, joint interventions, and tissue substitutes.

Rather than leaving prior authorization decisions to clinicians, CMS contracts third-party private vendors to manage automated algorithmic reviews that determine whether a physician's prescribed procedure meets "medical necessity."

Why Representative Suzan DelBene is Fighting WISeR

Congresswoman Suzan DelBene (WA-01) is spearheading congressional action and oversight to halt and repeal the WISeR model based on testimony from patients and medical providers across the nation:

  • Perverse Profit Incentives: Private contractors operating WISeR algorithms earn financial compensation structured around "averted medical costs," creating an inherent financial incentive to issue automated rejections.
  • Disruption & Delay of Essential Care: Algorithmic rejections subject patients to severe delays, multi-week appeals processes, and canceled surgeries—forcing individuals to live in prolonged pain or face deteriorating health.
  • Overriding Board-Certified Physicians: WISeR overrides individual treatment plans established by licensed doctors who personally examine patients, replacing medical judgment with standardized algorithmic formulas.
  • Lack of Transparency & Oversight: The proprietary algorithms used by WISeR vendors lack clinical transparency, making it difficult for patients and doctors to understand why coverage was rejected or delayed.
